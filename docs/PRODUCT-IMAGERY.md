# Dish serving concepts

Fourteen individual photos were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the unbuilt Munchy concept. The original PNGs remain in the Codex generated_images directory. Optimized copies are saved in public/images as WebP, with each filename below. They are fictional serving visualizations, not photos of an operating restaurant's dishes. Product captions distinguish generated serving concepts from illustrative stock photographs.

## Prompt set

The common prompt asked for one realistic individual editorial food photograph, landscape 4:3, natural daylight, real food textures, a modest Casablanca snack setting, warm wood or paprika tile, cream enamel/ceramic tableware and kraft paper where appropriate. No text, collage, watermarks or hands. Each following subject was generated with a separate tool call:

- taco-chicken-concept.webp: closed grilled Moroccan French taco with crispy chicken, fries and cheese sauce, cut cross section and garlic sauce pot.
- kefta-burger-concept.webp: single thick cumin kefta patty, grilled onions, tomato, harissa mayo and sesame bun.
- classic-smash-concept.webp: single thin crusty smashed beef patty, cheddar, pickles and house sauce in a smooth brioche bun.
- taco-kefta-concept.webp: rectangular grilled French taco with spiced kefta, fries and cheese sauce, no lettuce.
- taco-mixte-concept.webp: mixed chicken and ground beef French taco, fries and cheese sauce, cut cross section.
- pizza-poulet-concept.webp: individual chicken pizza with mozzarella, peppers and tomato sauce; one slice slightly separated.
- pizza-kefta-concept.webp: individual ground kefta pizza with onions, peppers, mozzarella and tomato sauce; overhead with one slice missing.
- panini-kefta-concept.webp: ridged pressed panini sandwich with kefta, onions and melted cheese, two diagonally cut halves.

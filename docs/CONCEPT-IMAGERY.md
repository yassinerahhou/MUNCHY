# Munchy restaurant concept imagery

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then converted to responsive WebP assets in this project. Both show a fictional future restaurant/team, not an existing establishment or actual employees. Keep the concept labels visible until replaced with real business photography.

## Restaurant space

Saved asset: `public/images/restaurant-concept.webp`.

Prompt: Create one premium photorealistic architectural lifestyle photograph, landscape 3:2, for the imagined MUNCHY neighborhood snack restaurant in Maarif Casablanca Morocco. Camera from inside looking across a compact real-world accessible casual snack space: warm cream plaster, paprika red tiled counter, brushed stainless open griddle kitchen with extraction hood, honey wood benches, small saffron accents, authentic Moroccan contemporary urban details, soft daylight from street window. Three fictional Moroccan adult workers wearing clean cream aprons and dark red tee shirts, one at counter smiling naturally and two preparing food hygienically in back, credible candid poses, no celebrities. Include small restrained MUNCHY lettering on wall. Lived-in but impeccably clean, affordable neighborhood restaurant raised to editorial quality, not palatial, not a generic US diner, no impossible architecture, no cinematic smoke. Realistic hands and faces. Natural 35mm professional hospitality photography. This is explicitly an architectural concept visualization for an unbuilt fictional brand. No captions, no collage, no stars, no watermarks.

## Comparison reference

Allo Simo Food was inspected in-browser at https://allosimofood.ma/home. Observed features: location selection, text search, sorting, vegetarian/non-vegetarian/popular/new/discount/halal filters, nearby discovery, trending/popular/best-reviewed tabs, restaurant service-type filters, support and tracking links. Its default Mohammédia location returned “No restaurant found”; no exact product menu or competitor recipe/pricing was claimed or copied.

Munchy uses a single restaurant model. The implemented adaptations are persistent category controls, smooth movement to filtered results, dietary/budget filters, expanded Moroccan snack categories, service-area information, a catering inbox and a managed storefront.
